OCR Magic reads images and picture-only Word files, rebuilds the paragraphs and chapters, and hands you back a clean PDF, Word or text file. It all happens on your own computer.

Installs in seconds from Chrome or Edge · Windows and Mac

OCR Magic is installed. It has opened in its own window, and from now on you will find it with your other apps. You can close this tab.

Picture-only Word files to PDF

Drop a .docx that is just page photos. Every page is read and stitched back into flowing text with headings and paragraphs.

Private by design

The reading engine runs inside the app. Your files never leave your computer and no AI service is involved.

Works offline

Once installed it opens from your desktop like any other app, with or without an internet connection.
